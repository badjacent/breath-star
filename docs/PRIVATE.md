# Material that isn't in this repository

Some documents and comments refer to material kept in a private registry beside this repository:

- **Lesson encodings** drawn from recorded lessons, with their step-by-step records. The recordings
  belong to their publishers, so their encodings are not published. The model runs any of them
  through `breathstar lesson --file PATH`; the format is in `Sources/BreathStar/Anatomy/LessonFile.swift`.
- **The working log and work plan:** every experiment, dated, including the ones on those lessons.
  `docs/RESULTS.md` and `docs/STATUS.md` carry what stands.
- **Independent reviews** of the model (2026-11-09 and 2026-11-10). Their open points are in
  `docs/STATUS.md`.
- **The original design plan** (2026-11-09), superseded by `docs/DESIGN.md` and
  `docs/LESSON_ADAPTATION.md`.

References to these appear as labels, for example "(working log, private)" or "(a review,
private)". They don't resolve outside the author's machine. Nothing needed to build, run or test the
model is private.
